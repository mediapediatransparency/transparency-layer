Which articles, and why

Chosen by a fixed rule, not by hand

So that the selection does not depend on what anyone expects to find, articles are picked by the same four steps in every topic area.

The rule

Four steps, the same for every area.

Step 1

Wikipedia marks contested subjects

A notice on an article's talk page puts it under special editing rules for a contentious topic area. For a few subjects Wikipedia does not mark, a WikiProject's own tags are used instead.

22

topic areas

Step 2

Sorted by kind of subject

Journalist or commentator, other public figure, news outlet, organisation, idea or movement, event; from Wikidata. Lists and articles that fit none are left out.

6

kinds of subject

Step 3

The most read are chosen

In each area, for each kind, the 25 articles read most over the previous 60 days. A few are added on request, and marked.

1,880

articles chosen

Step 4

Analysed in batches

Each chosen article is analysed from one exact version, with the same code and rules as every other.

1,877

analysed so far
