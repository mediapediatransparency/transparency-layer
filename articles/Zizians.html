How the summary rows are calculated. Opinion and harder-to-check citations

Of the article's 68 citations: not in English 3 (4%) · not in English, with no quote or translation 3 (4%) · behind a paywall or registration 6 (9%). We spot opinion pieces from the web address (such as /opinion/ or a blog) or from the footnote's own label. A book counts as not in English when the footnote says so, or when Open Library lists the book only in other languages. No quote or translation means the footnote does not copy out the passage it relies on, so a reader who does not know the language cannot check it.
