Wikipedia's voice in the opening

Each sentence of the opening section checked against Wikipedia's neutrality policy. A sentence is marked (orange edge) when there is evidence it is contested (editors took it out for a day or more and put it back, tagged it as disputed, or argued over it on the talk page; or it uses a word from Wikipedia's own “Words to watch” guideline) and it is stated in Wikipedia's voice, naming no one who holds the view.
