Second Kurdish–Iraqi War was the second chapter of the Barzani rebellion, initiated by the collapse of the Kurdish autonomy talks and the consequent Iraqi offensive against rebel KDP troops of Mustafa Barzani during 1974–1975. How the summary rows are calculated. For the sources in scope that have a score s (independent quality, 0 to 1; or audience leaning, −1 to +1), each cited c times: mean per source = (s1 + … + sn) ÷ n, each source counted once; mean per citation = (c1·s1 + … + cn·sn) ÷ (c1 + … + cn), each source counted as often as it is cited.
