Otzma Yehudit (, lit., Jewish Strength) is a far-right political party in Israel, and it has been referred to as "Israel's alt-right". It was originally formed as Otzma LeYisrael (, lit., Strength for Israel), or Strong Israel, and was established on 13 November 2012 by MKs Aryeh Eldad and Michael Ben-Ari, who split from the National Union to form a new party ahead of the 2013 elections.
