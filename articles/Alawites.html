Short description: none

The Alawis, also rendered as Alawites ( Alawiyyah/Alawīyah), are a sect of the Ghulat branch of Shia Islam, primarily centred in Syria. The eponymously-named Alawites revere Ali (Ali ibn Abi Talib), considered the first Imam of the Twelver school. However, they are generally considered to be ghulat by Shia Islam. The group is believed to have been founded by Ibn Nusayr during the 9th century and fully established as a religion. For this reason, Alawites are sometimes called Nusayris ( Nuṣayrīyyah), though the term has come to be used as a pejorative in the modern era. Another name, "Ansari" ( Anṣāriyyah), is believed to be a mistransliteration of "Nusayri".

The Alawis, Alawites ( Alawīyah), or pejoratively Nusayris ( Nuṣayrīyah) are an ethnoreligious group that lives primarily in the Levant and follows Alawism, a sect of Islam that originated from Shia Islam. The Alawites venerate Ali ibn Abi Talib, revered as the first Imam in the Twelver school, as the physical manifestation of God. The group is believed to have been founded by Ibn Nusayr during the 9th century. Ibn Nusayr was a disciple of the tenth Twelver Imam, Ali al-Hadi and of the eleventh Twelver Imam, Hasan al-Askari. For this reason, Alawites are also called Nusayris.
