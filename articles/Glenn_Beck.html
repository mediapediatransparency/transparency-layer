Glenn Lee Beck (born February 10, 1964) is an American television and radio host, political commentator, author, television network producer, libertarian, filmmaker and entrepreneur. Beck's last daily show on the network was June 30, 2011. In 2012, The Hollywood Reporter named Beck on its Digital Power Fifty list.
