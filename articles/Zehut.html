Zehut () is a right-libertarian and nationalist political party in Israel founded in 2015 by Moshe Feiglin. Its platform is centered on promoting individual liberty, including economic freedom, and the legalization of cannabis. The party also supports the Israeli annexation of the West Bank. The party won a Knesset seat in the 2026 Israeli legislative election in an alliance with the Religious Zionist Party, having previously come close to surpassing the 3.25% electoral threshold in the April 2019 Israeli legislative election, where it garnered a 2.7% share of over 100,000 votes.
