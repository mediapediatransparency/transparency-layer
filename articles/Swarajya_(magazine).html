Swarajya was a weekly magazine founded in 1956 by Khasa Subba Rao. C. Rajagopalachari one of the founders of the Swatantra Party, was a regular contributor to the magazine in the form of his 'Dear Reader' column. The magazine strongly advocated individual freedom and freedom of enterprise as against Nehru's socialist policies. Swarajya is an Indian monthly print magazine and online daily. It was a weekly magazine founded in 1956 by Khasa Subba Rao with the patronage of C.
