About the ratings: Wikipedia's own list (Perennial sources) rates about 530 sources that its editors argue about often. Most sources are not on it; that is not a bad mark.

Out of every 100 people sharing it, Republicans = (1 + score) ÷ 2 × 100, so −0.4 is about 70 Democrats to 30 Republicans. It comes from US Twitter users matched to voter records (DomainDemo, 2025; Robertson and colleagues, 2018) and covers only websites shared widely enough.
