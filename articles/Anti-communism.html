Short description: Opposition to communism

Anti-communism is political and ideological opposition to communism. Organized anti-communism developed after the 1917 October Revolution in the Russian Empire, and it reached global dimensions during the Cold War, when the United States and the Soviet Union engaged in an intense rivalry. Various political movements, not only including (most commonly) conservatism, fascism, liberalism, nationalism, social democracy, libertarianism, but including (very rarely) anarchism, socialism, and Leftism, possess anti-communist elements. Anti-communism has also been expressed in philosophy, by several religious groups, and in literature. Some well-known proponents of anti-communism are former communists. Anti-communism has also been prominent among movements resisting communist governance.
