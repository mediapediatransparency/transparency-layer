Short description: Ruling political party of Russia

The All-Russian Political Party "United Russia" is the ruling political party of Russia since 2001. As the largest party in the Russian Federation, it holds 349 (or 78%) of the 450 seats in the State Duma , having constituted the majority in the chamber since 2007. Its nationalist and Eurosceptic ideology is inconsistent and embraces specific officials, all of whom support Vladimir Putin. Although in 2009 it proclaimed Russian conservatism as its official ideology, it appeals mainly to pro-Putin and non-ideological voters, and is often classified by political scientists as a "big-tent party", or as a "party of power", rather than an organisation that is primarily based upon a political ideology.

How the summary rows are calculated. For the sources in scope that have a score s (independent quality, 0 to 1; or audience leaning, −1 to +1), each cited c times: mean per source = (s1 + … + sn) ÷ n, each source counted once; mean per citation = (c1·s1 + … + cn·sn) ÷ (c1 + … + cn), each source counted as often as it is cited.
