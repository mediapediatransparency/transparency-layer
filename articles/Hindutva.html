Hindutva ("Hinduness"), a term popularised by Vinayak Damodar Savarkar in 1923, it is the predominant form of Hindu nationalism in India. It is championed by the Hindu nationalist volunteer organisation Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS) Vishva Hindu Parishad and Hindu Sena. Many Indian social scientists have described the Hindutva movement as fascist, adhering to the concept of homogenised majority and cultural hegemony but some other Indian social scientists dispute this description.
