The Serbian Radical Party (, abbr. SRS) is a far-right ultranationalist political party in Serbia. Founded in 1991, its co-founder, first and only leader is Vojislav Šešelj. During the Yugoslav Wars, it became prominent, enjoying the status of the second largest party in the Serbian parliament under the regime of Slobodan Milošević. In this period and in the following decade, it had been described as neo-fascist.
