The Joint List (; ) is a political alliance of Arab-majority political parties in Israel. The current Joint List reestablished in 2026 by Hadash, Balad and Ta'al adheres to a left-wing, progressive and Anti-Zionist platform. Originally it included the Islamist Ra'am party ahead of the 2015 election, but the original Joint List was dissolved in 2019 and 2021 due to Ta'al and Ra'am withdrawing, respectively.
