How the summary rows are calculated. For the sources in scope that have a score s (independent quality, 0 to 1; or audience leaning, −1 to +1), each cited c times: mean per source = (s1 + … + sn) ÷ n, each source counted once; mean per citation = (c1·s1 + … + cn·sn) ÷ (c1 + … + cn), each source counted as often as it is cited. Opinion and harder-to-check citations

Of the article's 71 citations: opinion, comment or blog section 4 (6%) · no web link 3 (4%) · behind a paywall or registration 2 (3%). We spot opinion pieces from the web address (such as /opinion/ or a blog) or from the footnote's own label. A book counts as not in English when the footnote says so, or when Open Library lists the book only in other languages.
