The camp was initially intended to intern the Nazi Party's political opponents, which consisted of communists, social democrats, and other dissidents. It was located on the grounds of an abandoned munitions factory northeast of the medieval town of Dachau, about northwest of Munich in the Gau Munich-Upper Bavaria, in Southern Germany. The Dachau camp system grew to include nearly one hundred sub-camps , which were mostly work camps or arbeitskommandos, and were located throughout southern Germany and Austria. The main camp was liberated by U.S. forces on 29 April 1945. Dachau was the third concentration camp to be liberated by British or American Allied forces.
