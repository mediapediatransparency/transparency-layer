Academic Ranking of World Universities (ARWU), also known as the Shanghai Ranking, is annual university rankings published by Shanghai Ranking Consultancy. The ranking was first compiled by Shanghai Jiaotong University in 2003, the first of its kind (the first multi-indicator ranking). It is backed by the Chinese government to provide a global benchmark for Chinese universities to make progression and to catch up on hard scientific research. The publication now comprises world's overall and subject league tables, together with regional Greater China Ranking and Macedonian HEIs Ranking. Its claimed consistent and objective methodology is praised when compared with other rankings. Academic Ranking of World Universities is considered as one of the three most influential and widely observed international university rankings, along with the QS World University Rankings and Times Higher Education World University Rankings.
