Vladimir Ilyich Ulyanov (), alias Lenin (; ) (&nbsp;– 21 January 1924) was a Russian communist revolutionary, politician and political theorist. He served as head of government of the Russian Soviet Federative Socialist Republic from 1917, and of the Soviet Union from 1922 until his death.
