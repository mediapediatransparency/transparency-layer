Leaning of the sources

Who shares the websites each article cites

Researchers have measured, for tens of thousands of websites, whether the people who share them on social media are mostly Democrats or mostly Republicans. Here is where each article's cited websites fall on that measure. It describes who shares a site, not what it publishes, and it is not applied to books or journals.

Each article on one scale

Each dot is one article: the average for the websites it cites (DomainDemo, 2025). Left means the people sharing those sites are mostly Democrats, right mostly Republicans. The dark tick is the middle article of each area; the dashed line is the average across everything cited. Click a dot to open the article.

Only articles where at least 10% of citations go to rated websites are drawn; many articles rest mostly on books, which no open dataset rates. Most large news sites fall left of the middle on this measure, so compare with the average line.
