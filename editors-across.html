How it is worked out

Each word of today's text is credited to the editor who first added it (WikiWho); each citation to the editor who added it. An editor is shown if they wrote at least 1% of analysed articles in 2 or more topic areas; the 40 with the most such articles are listed. Areas follow Wikipedia's own contentious-topic notices and WikiProject banners. No account names, no personal details and nothing from outside Wikipedia is used.
